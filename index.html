About

Trained in the edit bay, raised on set — nine years across every seat in a production.
Deep DuhariaMohali, IN

Deep is a technical artist and film director bridging traditional filmmaking with next‑generation content creation — leading post‑production on music videos and short films, and increasingly directing with generative tools alongside a camera.

He started in 2016 animating motion graphics at Ssl Computers, moved through editing and poster design at VK Films and Los Pro Music, assisted directors Yaadu Brar and Jashan Nanarh on set, and now works freelance out of Vision Studio — directing, shooting and grading everything from commercials to full music‑video campaigns.

Most recently he's been pairing advanced lens science and lighting control with AI prompt engineering and generative video, using both to keep the story — not the tool — in charge of the frame.
